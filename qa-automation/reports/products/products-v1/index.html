What is going on
Frontend

The search compares the typed text with the product name only. “lamp”, “LAMP” and “ lamp ” all find Desk Lamp, so trimming and case are handled; the category is simply not part of the match. Our reading: the filter in the product list does not look at the category field.

Backend — nothing to do

The recording shows no request while searching: the only traffic is the page, its script and its stylesheet. The catalogue is in the page and filtered in the browser.

Steps
1. Log in as demo@example.com / password123.
2. On Products, type Home in “Search products”.
3. Leave the category on “All categories”.
Expected
Coffee Mug, Desk Lamp and Indoor Plant are listed and the count reads “Showing 3 of 12 products” (owner's rule R4, 19 Oct 2026: search matches the product name or its category).
Actual
No cards, the message No products found and the count Showing 0 of 12 products. Choosing category Home, then All categories, with “Home” still in the box, keeps “Showing 0 of 12 products”.
Impact
A shopper who searches by what kind of product they want, rather than its exact name, is told the shop has nothing and may leave. The category select still works, so the products are reachable, but only if the shopper thinks to use it.
